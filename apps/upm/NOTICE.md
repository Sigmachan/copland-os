# Copland UPM provenance

Copland UPM is a combined and modified work distributed under
AGPL-3.0-or-later.

- The pacman/AUR transaction engine is derived from Paru, copyright
  Morganamilo and Paru contributors, originally distributed under GPL-3.0.
- The high-level command vocabulary and recipe model are derived from and
  inspired by `merclamp/upm`, originally distributed under
  AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Compatibility behavior is inspired by Etersoft EPM (`Etersoft/eepm`),
  copyright Etersoft contributors, distributed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

The project is substantially modified for Copland OS. The original projects
do not endorse this fork and are not responsible for it.
